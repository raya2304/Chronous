01

The Mechanics

Three Ways To Measure Time

01

Mechanical

Powered by a mainspring and a complex system of gears, a mechanical watch represents traditional watchmaking at its most intricate.

02

Automatic

An automatic movement winds itself using the natural movement of the wearer's wrist.

03

Quartz

Quartz movements use an electronic oscillator regulated by a quartz crystal to provide highly accurate timekeeping.

A Brief History

The Evolution of Time

1500s

The Birth of Portable Time

Early portable clocks allowed people to carry timekeeping devices with them, laying the foundation for the development of watches.

1800s

Watches Become Personal

Watchmaking developed rapidly, and portable timepieces became increasingly associated with elegance, craftsmanship and status.

1900s

The Wristwatch Era

Wristwatches became increasingly popular and evolved into specialized designs for aviation, diving, sport and everyday wear.

Today

Tradition Meets Technology

Modern watchmaking combines centuries-old craftsmanship with advanced materials, precision engineering and contemporary design.
